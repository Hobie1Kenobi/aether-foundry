# THREAT — Missions

AI-assisted notes, not an audit.

The public routes cannot kill, approve, sign, or submit. A bearer on `POST /api/missions` is still `PUBLIC_WRITE`. The internal token is compared in memory and is not appended to the ledger.

Caps are integer drops. A restart folds the same log. Uncertain and bridge-pending holds are not released and are not retried. A failed transaction keeps its fee in the spent bucket. Account reserve is not spendable and is not a fee.

House addresses and synthetic accounts cannot become foreign revenue. Mainnet network ids and hosts used by the bridge status helper are refused. Non-XRP amounts and destination-chain gas stop the plan.

`SPENDING_ENABLED` is a constant false. There is no environment variable that turns it on.
